Case Study 01: Hypergrowth Scaling

Expanding Technical Headcount from 140 to 325 Post-Series B

Workforce Planning Talent Acquisition
Challenge:

Severe engineering talent shortage jeopardized the product roadmap. Existing agency-dependent hiring incurred $38,000+ per hire with a 68-day time-to-fill.

Intervention:

Established an in-house recruitment engine, implemented structured competency-based interviewing, and redesigned onboarding with a 30-60-90 day milestone system.

Measured Impact:
  • • Time-to-fill slashed from 68 to 37 days.
  • • Saved $420,000+ in agency commissions.
  • • 94% 90-day retention rate for technical cohorts.
Case Study 02: Performance Transformation

Overhauling Archaic Appraisals to Stem High-Performer Churn

Total Rewards Retention
Challenge:

A 29% attrition rate among Senior Engineers caused by opaque promotions, lack of clear progression, and annual, backward-looking reviews.

Intervention:

Designed a dual-track career progression matrix (Individual Contributor vs. People Manager). Rolled out quarterly agile performance reviews and manager calibration sessions.

Measured Impact:
  • • Regrettable attrition dropped from 29% to 11%.
  • • Internal promotions surged by 41%.
  • • Manager feedback rating increased from 61% to 89%.
